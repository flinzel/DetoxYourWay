What the app is for

Detox Your Way is for adults aged 18 and over who want to try voluntary changes in everyday life. You decide what fits your circumstances and whether to continue. The app does not diagnose or treat illness and does not provide instructions for withdrawing from substances. If you have symptoms, are experiencing dependence or are in an unsafe situation, seek qualified support locally.

Free use and Pro

Short experiments and selected week-long programmes are free. Pro adds more week-long programmes, month-long programmes and more detailed reflections. You need Pro to enter a new weight manually in the app. Connecting to Apple Health, importing weights and viewing your existing history are free. You can subscribe monthly or yearly. The App Store shows the price and billing period before you buy.

Renewal and cancellation

An auto-renewing subscription continues at the price and interval shown in the App Store until cancelled. Open Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings to check the renewal date and cancel. Observe the cancellation deadline shown there. Deleting the app or its entries does not cancel the subscription. Use Apple’s refund request process to ask for a refund.

Your entries

You control your personal entries. The privacy notice explains how local storage, Calendar export, sharing and the optional Apple Health connection work. Before sharing, check the preview and consider other people’s rights and privacy.

Licence and consumer rights

The linked Apple standard licence applies to software obtained through the App Store unless the store listing specifies another licence. The relevant storefront offer identifies the contracting party and the payment terms for a purchase. Mandatory legal protections, including applicable consumer guarantees and any statutory right of withdrawal, remain in effect.
