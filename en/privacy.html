Consumer health data privacy policy

Who is responsible?

The person named in the legal notice operates this website and provides Detox Your Way. Their postal address and email are listed in the contact details. You can use either to ask a privacy question.

When you visit this website

GitHub Pages serves these pages. Your browser sends connection and request details, including your IP address and the page requested, to the hosting service. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. The operator relies on its legitimate interest in delivering and securing the website under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The operator sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics tools on these pages. Fonts, images and styles are served with the site. An external service is contacted when you follow a link to it.

Hosting, recipients and international transfers

GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process data in the United States. GitHub’s linked privacy statement identifies the relevant companies, explains how retention is determined and describes the safeguards it uses for international transfers, including, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. It also explains how to contact GitHub about these safeguards. The operator keeps no separate visitor analytics.

What stays on your iPhone

The app has no app account or operator-run data server. Your iPhone stores plans, scheduled activities, attempts, reflections, optional notes, meals, purchase wishes, weights, language and adult-confirmation settings. Technical details may include the last sync or a purchase you started. The app does not send those personal entries to the operator or to advertising or analytics services. They are not used for targeted ads, credit scoring or automated decisions with legal effects.

Apple Health is optional

Body weight is the only Health data type requested. When you enable the relevant option and iOS permits access, the app can read available measurements with their time, source and identifier into your local history, or write your own measurements with a known time to Apple Health. Adding earlier measurements requires a separate deliberate action. You can switch reading and writing off independently. Depending on your Apple settings, Health data may sync through iCloud. The operator cannot use the app to retrieve Health measurements stored on your iPhone.

Deletion, backups and copies

Entries stay on your iPhone until you delete them or remove the app data. The app marks its private data store for exclusion from iPhone backups. Reinstallation or a change of device may result in lost entries. Removing an imported weight in the app leaves the measurement in Health. For a measurement the app wrote and can identify as its own, you can separately confirm deletion from Health. Clearing all app data stops local syncing. Existing Health or calendar records and items you have shared remain outside the app.

Notifications, Calendar and sharing

Optional reminders and the Lock Screen timer use iOS system features. Your weight and the source of a Health measurement do not appear there. You confirm calendar exports in Apple’s editor. When sharing, you choose the details in the preview and where to send the text, invitation or card. The calendar, messaging or other service you select handles the resulting copy under its own terms.

If you use Pro

Apple handles purchases and payments through the App Store. The app uses verified transaction and status information to provide or restore Pro access. The operator may receive sales or accounting reports from Apple. Personal journal entries and health information are not needed for billing. The legal basis for providing a purchased service is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Article 6(1)(c) applies where records must be kept by law.

If you email support

The operator and its email service, Gmail (Google), process your sender address, message and any details you choose to include so they can answer your question. For a contractual enquiry the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For other messages it is the operator’s legitimate interest in replying under Article 6(1)(f). Once the matter is resolved, correspondence is deleted when it is no longer needed for follow-up, legal claims or statutory record keeping. Contacting support is voluntary. A personal reply requires a return address. Google may process emails outside the EEA, including in the United States. Google’s “Legal frameworks for data transfers” explains the transfer mechanisms it uses in different circumstances and where to find further information.

Your privacy rights

Subject to the applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or data portability. Where processing relies on legitimate interests, you may object for reasons relating to your particular situation. You may withdraw consent at any time for future processing. You can also change body-weight permissions directly in iOS. Because the operator cannot retrieve your local entries, you can view and manage them on your iPhone.

Requests and complaints

Use the contact details to reach the operator. You may also complain to a data protection authority, particularly where you live or work, or where you believe an infringement took place. Additional rights under the laws that apply where you live are unaffected.
