Where do I start?

Open Discover and choose a topic. Read the suggestion and the alternative. Adjust the action to suit your day. Timed phone breaks also have a shorter option. Once you’ve made a plan, you’ll find your next experiment in Today.

Can I try having less contact with someone?

Yes. You can practise what to say the next time someone asks for a favour, or make time for someone you enjoy seeing. The Pro month-long programme also offers the option of taking a break from contact. You choose when it starts, which arrangements still need to stand and whether to get back in touch. If you’re afraid of the person’s reaction, you don’t have to test a confrontation.

What if I skip a day or change the plan?

When you look back, you choose how to describe what happened. Doing things differently and skipping an activity are both valid entries. You can leave the explanation blank. Your history lets you revisit what you actually tried.

How do reminders and Calendar work?

If you choose reminders, the app asks for permission to send notifications. You can change that permission in iPhone Settings. To put a plan in Calendar, confirm it in the calendar editor. If you later change or delete the plan in the app, check your calendar copy separately.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Open Apple Health from the weight chart. Choose whether you want to see weight measurements from Health or save your own measurements there. These are separate settings, each subject to iOS permission for body weight. If no measurements appear, the app cannot tell whether there are no measurements or its read access is limited. You can check permissions in the Health app, under your profile and the apps with access.

How do I delete my information?

Open an entry to remove it. To clear your local app data, use the option in the app’s settings. Removing an imported weight hides its local copy. To remove a measurement the app wrote to Health, confirm the separate Health deletion. Manage calendar copies, content you have already shared and your Apple subscription separately.

How do I manage Pro?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple shows your billing period, renewal date and cancellation options. If Pro is missing after reinstalling the app, restore purchases using the same Apple Account. This restores access to a valid purchase. It cannot recover your personal entries.

Need technical help?

Tell us which screen you were on and what you expected to happen. Your app version, iPhone model and iOS version are useful. Please leave out weight measurements, personal notes, payment information and screenshots containing health details. Support can help with the app. For medical questions, speak to a qualified healthcare professional. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
