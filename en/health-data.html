What information does the app use?

If you record your weight, the app keeps the measurement, unit, date and any time you provide. Entries about meals, calories, rest, habits and your own reflections may also reveal information about your health. You choose what to enter and can leave optional notes blank. There is no app account. The operator cannot look up these entries on a server.

Where does the information come from?

The sources are your own entries and, only if you enable reading and iOS permits it, weight measurements available through Apple Health. Those measurements can originate from another app or device you use. The app reads only body weight from Health, together with information needed to identify a measurement, such as its source, date, available time and record identifier.

Why is it used?

Your iPhone uses the entries to show your own history, draw the weight chart and let you correct records or look back on an experiment. Local identifiers, sync status and deletion markers help keep imports and corrections consistent. The operator does not sell consumer health data, use it for targeted advertising or infer a health profile from your entries.

Who receives information?

If you enable writing and allow access in iOS, the app can save your own measurements with a known time to Apple Health. Earlier measurements require a separate choice. Depending on your settings, Apple may sync Health data through iCloud or provide access to other apps you authorise. There are no affiliates to which the operator discloses your consumer health data. The operator does not send your measurements, their source or private journal entries to advertisers or analytics providers.

If you choose to share or contact support

Review the preview before sending an invitation or adding a calendar copy. Your chosen recipient or destination service receives the fields you select. Your weight and its Health source are not included in share cards, calendar exports or the Lock Screen timer. If you put health information in a support email yourself, the operator and the email service, Gmail (Google), receive the message. You do not need to provide measurements, diagnoses or notes to get technical help.

Control access and remove entries

You can use the app without connecting Health. Turn reading or writing off separately in the weight chart’s Apple Health settings. You can also withdraw the relevant permission in iOS. This stops future access in that direction. It does not remove existing copies. In the app, view or correct your own entry, remove a local record, or clear all local app data. A locally removed import remains in Health. To remove a measurement the app itself wrote from Health too, choose and confirm that separate action. Manage other measurements and external copies with their respective source.

Access, copies and deletion requests

You may ask the operator to confirm whether your health data was collected, shared or sold, to provide access or a copy of information the operator holds, to identify recipients and how to contact them, to withdraw consent, or to delete information the operator holds. Use the contact below and describe the request without including your health history. The operator may need limited information to verify the request. Entries that exist only on your iPhone can be accessed there; the operator cannot download or erase them remotely.

How to appeal a decision

If your request is declined, reply to the decision or write to the same address with the subject “Health privacy appeal”. Identify the decision you would like reviewed. You will receive a written explanation. Where Washington’s My Health My Data Act applies, the operator will respond to your appeal within 45 days. If it is denied, you may file a complaint with the Washington State Attorney General. Other complaint and privacy rights available where you live remain in effect.

Email support

Please leave health details out of support messages. If unnecessary health information arrives, we may ask for a redacted message and will remove the unnecessary details unless retention is legally required.
