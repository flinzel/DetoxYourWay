“Let me check my diary. I’ll get back to you tomorrow.”

That gives you time to decide. You can still say yes or no tomorrow.

Two minutes at the table

As you put your plate down, leave your phone out of reach. Take a bite while the food is still warm. If you’re expecting an important call, leave the sound on.

What if you need to take a call?

Leave the ringer on and turn your phone face down. What made a difference at dinner: the two minutes, or having the screen out of sight?
